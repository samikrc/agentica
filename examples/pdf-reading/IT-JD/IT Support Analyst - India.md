# Desktop Support Analyst, IT Operations (India)
## Responsibilities and Requirements

### Position Summary
The Desktop Support Analyst role will play a critical role within Quantum's global IT organization, partnering closely with IT Operations and the IT Global team to support end users, drive technology initiatives, and contribute to business success. The role is based in Bangalore, India, working in close coordination with the IT Operations team in Houston.

This role is an opportunity for those interested in diverse technology initiatives within the scope of innovative high-touch user support. The analyst will work with cloud technologies, modern workplace tools, digital transformation efforts, and non-functional IT projects.

Key requirements include: problem-solving, proactive, growth-oriented, and eager to grow into ever expanding responsibilities within IT Operations and systems administration.

### Job Responsibilities
- The Desktop Support Analyst's responsibilities are to provide end user IT support and service delivery to Quantum users over a variety of platforms; require extensive knowledge of IT infrastructure, as well as strong problem solving skills.
- Provide high quality customer support with a customer first mentality.
- Manage and resolve customer issues using ITIL/ITSM principles.
- Manage, support, and configure IT infrastructure for diverse hardware and user technologies and peripherals
- Support and manage Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android devices
- Provide standard hardware for enterprise IT systems.

---

- Escalate service requests that cannot be scheduled within agreed service levels.
- Responsible for entering time and expenses as occur.
- Enter all service tickets in Jira.
- Technical liaison with other teams as needed for remote support, printer support, replacement of equipment, assist with projects similar to this nature.
- Additional ownership of some project work may be requested as needed by the IT Director
- Consistently gain knowledge and/or certification in relevant or assigned technologies.
- Work collaboratively with your IT peers, and Quantum community to solve problems

# How do I use Even?

For consultations and lab tests (OPD)

Step 1

Book a consultation with us

Book a quick video consultation with our in-house doctors on the Even app, by calling our support number or texting your Even Care team. Our doctors will understand your symptoms & recommend the best course of treatment.

Step 2

We unlock the payment scanner

If the Even doctors recommend a second opinion or a test, we will unlock the Even payment scanner for you to use at the recommended facility. Wherever possible, we will arrange a home sample collection for you.

Step 3

We pay your bill

Use the Even payment scanner to scan the UPI code at the facility. Enter the amount and we'll pay for it then and there! If for some reason payments do not go through, we will reimburse you via the app.

Step 4

Share your docs

Upload bills, doctor notes, prescriptions, etc on the Even app to reactivate it for the next use.

Small emergencies?

Please click the phone button on the app to contact us directly for immediate care for cases such as a broken arm, foot etc.

Bear in mind that some of the terms of the policy document will be specified in your final Certificate of Insurance (Col), which will have your sum insured and the specific inclusions and exclusions. These will agree with what we have said in this document. In all cases of disputes, the policy documents and Col will take precedence.

TargetCo, Inc. ("TargetCo") is a leading provider of workflow automation software
serving mid-market enterprises in North America and Western Europe. Founded in 2012
and headquartered in Austin, Texas, TargetCo offers a subscription-based SaaS platform
with over 1,200 customers across manufacturing, logistics, and business services.

TargetCo's value proposition is centered on improving operational efficiency and
reducing manual processing time by 20–40% for its customers. The company generates
approximately 92% of its revenue from recurring subscription contracts with an
average remaining term of 2.3 years.

The private equity sponsor is evaluating a control investment in TargetCo to support
continued product innovation, international expansion, and selective M&A.

TargetCo Q1 2023 Earnings Call Excerpt

CEO:
"In the first quarter, we continued to see strong demand from both new and existing
customers. Net revenue retention remained above 115%, driven by seat expansion and
adoption of our advanced analytics module. We also launched an AI-powered workflow
recommendation engine, which we believe will be a key differentiator going forward."

CFO:
"From a profitability standpoint, we remain focused on balancing growth and margins.
Our adjusted EBITDA margin in Q1 2023 was 25.8%, broadly in line with our full-year
2022 performance, as we continued to invest in sales capacity and R&D while driving
efficiencies in our cloud infrastructure spend."

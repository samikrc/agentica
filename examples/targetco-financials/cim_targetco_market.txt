TargetCo operates in a large and growing addressable market. The global workflow
automation software market is estimated at $8.5 billion in 2023 and is expected to
grow at a 14% CAGR through 2028, driven by continued digitization and labour cost
inflation.

Competitive dynamics are rational, with a mix of legacy on-premise software vendors
and newer cloud-native players. TargetCo has a defensible position in the mid-market
segment, with high switching costs due to workflow configuration, user training,
and integrations with core ERP systems.

Key growth drivers for TargetCo include:
- Expansion of seat count within existing customers
- Upsell of advanced analytics and AI-based modules
- Geographic expansion into Western Europe and APAC
- Targeted acquisitions of smaller competitors

# TargetCo, Inc. — Investment Analysis Example

## Context

TargetCo is a workflow automation SaaS company being evaluated for a control investment by a private equity sponsor. This directory contains due diligence materials including a Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM), financial statements, and management commentary.

## Analytical Questions

Use the files in this directory to answer the following:

1. **Revenue Quality & Sustainability**
   - What is driving the deceleration in revenue growth from 23.4% (2022) to 16.9% (2023E)?
   - Is the 115%+ NRR sustainable given the market maturity and competitive dynamics?

2. **Margin Expansion Trajectory**
   - EBITDA margins improved 400bps from 2021 to 2022, but only 100bps expected in 2023.
   - What specific investments are absorbing operating leverage, and when should margin expansion resume?

3. **Capital Efficiency**
   - CapEx has run at ~4.5% of revenue. Is this sufficient for a SaaS business claiming AI/ML differentiation?
   - How does this compare to peer cloud-native workflow automation vendors?

4. **Growth Optionality**
   - Management cites four growth levers: seat expansion, upsell (analytics/AI), geographic expansion, and M&A.
   - Which of these is most credible given the current customer base and competitive positioning?

5. **Valuation Benchmarking**
   - At an assumed 12x EBITDA entry multiple, what revenue growth and margin profile is required over a 5-year hold to achieve a 2.5x MOIC?
   - What are the key risks to achieving these targets?

6. **Management Incentives & Alignment**
   - The CFO emphasizes "balancing growth and margins" while the CEO focuses on "strong demand" and AI differentiation.
   - What does this divergence in messaging suggest about near-term vs. long-term priorities?
